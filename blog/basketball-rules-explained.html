Basketball Basics: The Rules Every New Fan Should Know

By Nadia Petrova · June 22, 2026

New to basketball? A quick tour of the core rules, from the shot clock to fouls, turns a confusing scramble into a game you can actually follow.

Basketball can look like controlled chaos to a newcomer, but a handful of core rules make the game easy to follow. At its simplest, two teams of five players each try to score by shooting a ball through the opposing team's hoop.

Scoring comes in three values. A basket made from inside the three-point line is worth two points, one from beyond it is worth three, and a free throw, awarded after certain fouls, is worth one. The team with the most points when time runs out wins.

The shot clock keeps the game moving. A team has a limited number of seconds to attempt a shot that hits the rim, or they lose possession. This rule prevents teams from stalling and forces constant action.

Dribbling rules shape how players move. A player must bounce the ball while moving; stopping the dribble and then starting again is a 'double dribble,' and moving without dribbling is 'traveling.' Both result in a turnover.

Fouls govern contact. Illegal physical contact gives the other team free throws or possession, and players who accumulate too many fouls are removed from the game. Understanding these basics is enough to start enjoying any match.
