Car Maintenance Basics Every Driver Should Know

By Grace Lindqvist · July 14, 2026

A little routine care keeps a car reliable and safe for years. These simple checks can save you money and prevent breakdowns at the worst moments.

You do not need to be a mechanic to keep a car healthy. A handful of simple, regular checks go a long way toward preventing expensive repairs and dangerous breakdowns. The owner's manual is the best starting point for any specific vehicle.

Engine oil is the lifeblood of the car. Check the level regularly using the dipstick, and change the oil at the interval recommended by the manufacturer. Clean oil keeps the engine running smoothly and extends its life dramatically.

Tyres deserve attention too. Check the pressure monthly and before long trips, since underinflated tyres wear faster, use more fuel, and handle poorly. Inspect the tread depth and look for uneven wear, which can signal alignment problems.

Brakes, lights, and fluids round out the basics. Listen for squealing or grinding when braking, make sure all exterior lights work, and keep coolant, brake fluid, and windscreen washer fluid topped up. These quick checks take minutes but matter enormously for safety.

Finally, do not ignore warning lights or unusual noises. Catching a small problem early is almost always cheaper and safer than waiting for it to become a major failure. Regular servicing by a trusted mechanic ties all of this together.
