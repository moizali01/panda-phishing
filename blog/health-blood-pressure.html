High Blood Pressure: The Silent Risk You Can Control

By Dr. Helen Asante · August 20, 2026

Often called the 'silent killer,' high blood pressure usually has no symptoms yet raises the risk of heart attack and stroke. Here is what everyone should know.

Blood pressure is the force of blood pushing against the walls of your arteries. When that force stays too high over time, the condition is called hypertension, or high blood pressure. It is one of the most common chronic conditions in the world.

Hypertension is often called the 'silent killer' because it typically produces no obvious symptoms. Many people feel completely well while their blood pressure quietly damages their arteries, heart, and other organs. The only reliable way to know is to measure it.

Readings are given as two numbers, systolic over diastolic. In general, a reading consistently at or above 130/80 is considered elevated or high, though exact thresholds and targets should be interpreted by a healthcare professional based on individual circumstances.

Lifestyle changes are the foundation of control: reducing salt intake, eating plenty of fruits and vegetables, staying physically active, limiting alcohol, not smoking, and managing stress. When lifestyle changes are not enough, effective medications are available.

Because hypertension has no warning signs, regular monitoring is the key defense. Many pharmacies and clinics offer free checks. This article is general information only. Anyone concerned about their blood pressure should consult a qualified medical professional.
